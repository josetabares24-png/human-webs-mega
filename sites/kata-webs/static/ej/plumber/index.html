Good things
to know.

Water is coming out right now. What first?

If it is safe, close the water shutoff valve. Stay away from wet electrical outlets. Call emergency services if there is immediate danger.

Can you give a price over the phone?

A photo and description help narrow it down. The final repair scope needs an inspection; approve the estimate before work begins.
