## Atomix (atomixnyc.com) — 2026-10-31
Premium independent Korean tasting-menu restaurant in NYC (Squarespace).

**Observed:**
- Structure: Minimal horizontal nav (COLLABS, CHEF'S COUNTER, BAR TASTING, ABOUT US). Hero is mostly photography + logo. Main CTAs are "BOOK NOW" and "CURRENT MENU". Sparse pages with short paragraphs and practical notes.
- Typography: Logo/headings use Ambroise Std (serif, ~19px, weight 400, letter-spacing 5.7px, uppercase). Body is Heebo (sans, 15px, weight 300, line-height 34.5px ≈ 2.3). Subheads sometimes Work Sans. High tracking on nav and buttons.
- Buttons: Sharp rectangles (border-radius 0), filled deep indigo rgb(33, 33, 192), white text, padding ~13px 26px, letter-spacing 3.5px, Heebo. No outlines or text-only primary CTAs. High contrast, editorial.
- Colors: Pure white bg rgb(255,255,255), near-black text rgb(26,26,26) or rgb(5,5,5). Accent is the indigo on buttons. No gradients, no secondary colors dominating.
- Spacing / borders / shadows / photography: Almost no box-shadows or borders. Images are large, high-quality professional food/interior shots (credited, Squarespace CDN), object-fit fill, no frames. Generous vertical rhythm from line-height; containers have light horizontal padding (~17px). Clean, airy.
- Menu / mobile / copy: Menu is linked externally or PDF-style. Dietary restrictions stated bluntly ("not able to accommodate vegan..."). Copy is restrained: "NEW KOREAN CUISINE", short origin story, practical email for wine list or inquiries. Awards listed factually (Michelin 2-stars, etc.). Mobile likely collapses nav (Squarespace default); desktop is clean horizontal.

**4-6 concrete patterns for anti-AI webs:**
1. Letter-spacing as luxury signal: 3.5–5.7px tracking on nav, logo, and CTAs creates deliberate, non-default rhythm that AI generators rarely apply consistently.
2. Weight contrast within one sans: body at 300 with 2.3 line-height vs buttons at regular weight + tracking — feels editorial, not system-ui default.
3. Sharp (0 radius) filled indigo buttons on white — avoids the rounded-pill / soft-shadow aesthetic that dominates AI sites.
4. Photography without chrome: large unbordered, unshadowed images carry the page; text is secondary and sparse. No stock-photo look or heavy overlays.
5. Honest, practical copy: explicit limitations (allergies, corkage $100, email for list) instead of marketing superlatives. Reads like a real operator, not a prompt.
6. Dual-font with custom serif for brand mark only: Ambroise Std used sparingly for logo/heading, Heebo everywhere else — restrained hierarchy instead of everything in one trendy sans.

These patterns favor restraint, specific tracking, and operational honesty over polished marketing defaults.

These patterns emphasize editorial typography pairing, text-based hierarchy, photographic calm, and operational specificity. Useful for anti-AI restaurant sites that should feel considered and human rather than template-polished.
