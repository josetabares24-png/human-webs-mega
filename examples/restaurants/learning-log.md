# Restaurant Learning Log

## 2026-11-07 — St. JOHN, Smithfield, London

**URL:** https://stjohnrestaurant.com/  
**Type:** Independent / premium-casual British (nose-to-tail, multi-site but family-run feel)

### What I saw (specific)

- **Structure & CTAs:** Simple text-first homepage. Short intro paragraph about beginning quietly. Sections for RESTAURANTS, BAKERIES, ABOUT, WINES, SHOP with “LEARN MORE” links. Top nav is text: RESTAURANTS, BAKERY, St. JOHN WINES, SHOP, EVENTS, RESERVATIONS. Mobile: MENU / CLOSE toggle. Practical contact in footer (phone, emails). No giant hero, no sticky CTA bar visible.
- **Typography:** Body uses “Adigi Times”, georgiapro, Georgia, Times, serif — 16px, weight 400, black. Nav and section titles uppercase, weight 700, ~18px. Classic serif throughout, no mixed sans. High contrast, traditional editorial feel.
- **Buttons:** Almost pure text links. Nav and “LEARN MORE” are uppercase text, no background, border-radius 0, minimal padding. Cookie banner has outline and solid black buttons (the only filled ones). No rounded filled primary CTAs.
- **Colors:** Pure white background, pure black text (rgb(0,0,0)). No accent colors. Extremely high contrast, monochrome.
- **Espaciado, bordes, sombras, fotografía:** Generous line-height (24px), ample whitespace. No box-shadows. Photography is real and sparse: bar interior, some food/event images, logo is simple SVG. Not a grid of hyper-stylized plates.
- **Menú:** On Smithfield page, daily menus (Lunch, Supper, Bar) as plain text lists with prices right next to items (e.g. “Roast Bone Marrow and Parsley Salad 16.50”). Option to DOWNLOAD PDF. Notes about allergens, corkage, no phones in dining room. Menus written twice a day.
- **Copy:** Highly specific and understated. “St. JOHN began quietly, cooking what we enjoyed for those who enjoyed what we do.” Mentions the derelict Georgian smokehouse, Smithfield Market, “no grand plan”, “gentle rigour”, nose-to-tail. Avoids any marketing superlatives.

### 5 patrones concretos útiles para webs anti-IA

1. **Serif editorial puro:** Georgia/Times family for everything. Feels like a printed menu or newspaper, not a geometric sans template. Instantly less “AI-generated” than Inter/Manrope everywhere.
2. **Menú diario en texto plano con precios inline:** Lista simple, precios al lado, actualizado el mismo día. Nada de cards con fotos de cada plato ni PDF como única opción.
3. **Botones = texto en mayúsculas:** Acciones principales son links uppercase sin relleno ni radio. Solo el banner de cookies tiene botones filled/outline. Contrasta con los primary buttons redondeados y coloridos de la mayoría de sitios modernos.
4. **Monocromo extremo + whitespace:** Blanco puro, negro puro, sin acentos. El diseño respira. La ausencia de color es una decisión estética deliberada, no un template genérico.
5. **Copy personal y concreto, sin fluff:** Nombra el edificio, el año, el mercado, “no grand plan”, “gentle rigour”. Se siente escrito por alguien que conoce el sitio, no generado para “contar una historia inspiradora”.

**Notas honestas:** Sitio WordPress (by Adigi) muy limpio y funcional. En móvil el menú se despliega bien. Hay un banner de cookies algo intrusivo, pero el resto es restraint total. Ideal referencia de estética pre-IA / anti-template: humano, útil, sin pretensiones visuales.

## 2026-11-07 — Padella, London (Borough Market, Shoreditch, Soho)

**URL:** https://www.padella.co/  
**Type:** Independent / casual pasta bars (multi-site, menu-first)

### What I saw (specific)

- **Structure & CTAs:** Homepage is the menu itself. Locations Borough Market / Shoreditch / Soho at top. Date “SATURDAY 7TH NOVEMBER”. Categorized text list (ANTIPASTI, PASTA, PUDDING, ICE CREAM) with item + price inline. Short About naming founders Tim and Jordan. Group Bookings with emails. Minimal nav. No giant hero, no sticky CTA.
- **Typography:** Body “Roboto Condensed”, sans-serif, 16px, weight 400, white. Headings Oswald 24–40px, weight 500–700. Condensed utilitarian sans. High contrast.
- **Buttons:** Almost none. Plain text links (transparent, radius 0). No filled primary CTAs.
- **Colors:** Pure black background rgb(0,0,0), pure white text. No accents. Extreme monochrome.
- **Espaciado, bordes, sombras, fotografía:** Functional menu spacing. No shadows. Sparse photos of locations. Menu is text, not food-porn.
- **Menú:** Homepage. Plain text with prices (e.g. “Pici cacio & pepe £13.5”, “Pappardelle with 8 hours Hereford beef shin ragu £16.5”). Date-stamped. No cards or dish photos.
- **Copy:** Direct. “Padella is the culmination of Tim and Jordan’s long held dream to open a pasta bar serving fresh, hand rolled pasta...” Mentions Trullo. Practical emails. No fluff.

### 5 patrones concretos útiles para webs anti-IA

1. **Homepage = menú del día en texto plano:** Lista categorizada con precios inline y fecha visible. La comida es el contenido principal.
2. **Fondo negro puro + sans condensado (Roboto Condensed + Oswald):** Alto contraste utilitario, como menú de pizarra.
3. **Cero botones filled:** Acciones son links de texto o emails. Reserva sin gritar.
4. **Copy que nombra fundadores y platos concretos:** Tim y Jordan, “hand rolled pasta”, ragu de 8 horas.
5. **Fotografía mínima y de lugar:** Prioriza texto del menú sobre grids de food photography.

**Notas honestas:** Sitio WordPress simple. Menú actualizado con fecha. En móvil legible. Práctico y on-brand para pasta bar casual. Ideal anti-IA por priorizar utilidad (qué hay hoy y cuánto cuesta) sobre estética de template.
