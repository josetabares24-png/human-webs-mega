## Noma (noma.dk) — premium independent, New Nordic fine dining / food lab, Copenhagen (René Redzepi)

Investigated live site (desktop) 5 November 2026. WordPress, quiet editorial with warm cream canvas and serif type. Currently focused on Copenhagen return and Noma LA residency.

### What was seen

- **Structure & CTAs**: Home opens with large heading “Copenhagen 2026-2027” and “Book a table”. Long narrative letter about coming home after LA residency, new leadership (Mette Brink Søberg, Pablo Soto, Annika de Las Heras), twelve seasons rhythm. Sections for Noma Projects shop, Noma Kaffe, book. Nav is minimal text (Reservations, Menu). Footer has contact (Refshalevej 96, phone, hours). No giant empty hero slogan; the letter is the content. Mobile would stack cleanly; “Book a table” remains visible.
- **Typography**: Body and headings: Reckless / Reckless-Neue (serif), body ~18px weight 400, headings 25–56px weight 400–500, color dark green-brown rgb(41,56,43). Some nav/UI labels use Roobert (sans). Strong literary serif feel; not geometric sans.
- **Buttons**: Mostly text links or sharp-corner (border-radius 0). Cookie “Accept all” is filled dark green-brown on cream. “Book a table” / “Reservations” are plain text or light. No heavy rounded filled CTAs or shadows. Contrast high via type.
- **Colors**: Warm cream/off-white background rgb(249,249,243). Dark green-brown text. No bright accents or gradients. Color lives in photography (ingredients, spaces, products).
- **Spacing / borders / shadows / photography**: Generous spacing around narrative blocks. Photos rectangular, border-radius 0, no box-shadow. Documentary of real places, ingredients, books, coffee. Feels like a journal or research lab announcement rather than restaurant marketing.
- **Menu / mobile / copy**: No static menu (seasonal, operational). Copy is highly specific and institutional: exact dates of LA residency (March 11–June 26), named new leaders and roles, “twelve seasons”, gratitude for foragers/ceramicists/fishermen, “It feels good to be coming home.” Language is precise, reflective, not generic hospitality filler. Contact and booking paths clear.

### Patterns useful for anti-IA webs

1. **Warm cream canvas + consistent literary serif (Reckless)** — book/journal feel on beige instead of white + Inter. Avoids AI template geometric sans + rounded everything.
2. **Typography and plain text as primary CTA** — “Book a table” as text or light link; sharp radius 0 on any filled elements. Quiet confidence over button chrome.
3. **Long specific narrative letter as homepage content** — detailed operational and leadership update with real names, dates, and reflections. Requires deep knowledge; not generatable from a generic prompt.
4. **Rectangular unshadowed documentary photography** — ingredients, spaces, products. Atmosphere from the real institution and research, not stock food close-ups.
5. **Minimal palette, color from photos** — cream + dark green-brown. Luxury/research signal from type, restraint, and specificity rather than decorative UI.
6. **Honest institutional voice** — naming new leaders, seasonal structure, past challenges, specific collaborators. Transparency and place/time-bound language.

Honesto: sitio muy quieto y seguro de su reputación. El tipo serif Reckless sobre crema lo hace sentir como un anuncio o carta institucional más que una web de restaurante template. La fotografía es de productos, ingredientes y espacios reales. Ideal referencia para premium independiente con voz fuerte que no caiga en el cliché AI de botones redondeados filled grandes, white space vacío y copy genérico de “experiencia culinaria única”.

## The French Laundry (thomaskeller.com/tfl) — premium independent, classic French-Californian fine dining, Yountville Napa Valley (Thomas Keller)

Investigated live site (desktop) 5 November 2026. Part of the Thomas Keller Restaurant Group site. Extremely restrained, institutional, almost archival. White background, tiny text, classic Emigre/Hoefler typefaces, navy accents. Homepage is mostly a large building photo + Michelin/Relais logos + text navigation. No flashy hero, no large CTAs.

### What was seen

- **Structure & CTAs**: Top navigation is text only (About, Reservations, Menus & Stories, Wine & Spirits, Private Dining & Events, Culinary Garden). Dropdowns expand for subpages. Primary action is external link to ExploreTock for reservations. Homepage has a wide photo of the stone building/courtyard, then logos. Subpages (e.g. Restaurant) contain historical narrative. No sticky bars, no big buttons above the fold. Mobile menu exists but design is desktop-oriented with small type.
- **Typography**: Headings and primary nav: Filosofia (Zuzana Licko / Emigre, Bodoni-inspired modern serif), ~17.4px, weight 400, color navy rgb(0,47,101), slight letter-spacing. Body text: Ideal Sans SSm (Hoefler & Co. humanist sans), ~10–11.6px, weight 300, color dark gray rgb(51,51,51). Sub-navigation uses Mrs Eaves (Emigre, Baskerville-inspired). Extremely small body size; feels like printed catalog or old website. High contrast but requires zoom or good eyesight.
- **Buttons**: Almost none. Everything is plain text links or expandable buttons with no background, no border-radius (or 1px), no padding, no shadows. Color is navy or gray. No filled, outline, or rounded pill CTAs. Reservations is just a text link to Tock. Quiet, almost invisible interaction design.
- **Colors**: Pure white background. Navy blue rgb(0,47,101) for headings and key links. Medium gray rgb(106,106,106) for secondary. Body dark gray. No accents, gradients, or warm tones on the UI. Color comes from the photography of the historic building, gardens, and (on menu page) food images. Very classical, institutional palette.
- **Spacing / borders / shadows / photography**: Tight, print-like spacing. Images have border-radius 0, no box-shadow. Photography is documentary: exterior of the 1900 river-rock building, Michelin stars, occasional food (chocolate tart). Feels archival rather than appetizing lifestyle. No full-bleed modern food porn.
- **Menu / mobile / copy**: Menu page lists only “Two tasting menus are offered daily: CHEF'S TASTING MENU / TASTING OF VEGETABLES” — no prices or full list (typical high-end). Copy on restaurant page is highly specific historical narrative: Keller finding the space in early 1990s, building constructed 1900 as saloon by Scottish stonemason, later French steam laundry, purchased by Schmitt in 1978, Keller in 1994. Mentions 1,600 sq ft, Michelin three stars since 2007 (only American with two), James Beard awards, Wine Spectator Grand Award, Relais & Châteaux. Language is factual, precise, not marketing fluff. Mobile: small type and nav may feel cramped; site prioritizes desktop elegance over thumb-friendly buttons.

### Patterns useful for anti-IA webs

1. **Tiny body text + classic high-quality typefaces (Filosofia + Ideal Sans + Mrs Eaves)** — print-catalog feel, 10–11px body. Forces attention and signals craftsmanship. Completely opposite of AI default 16–18px Inter/system-ui with generous line-height.
2. **Navy blue on pure white + zero decorative UI** — institutional, almost governmental or museum aesthetic. No rounded buttons, no shadows, no warm cream. Luxury through restraint and type quality rather than color or motion.
3. **Text-only navigation and CTAs** — reservations as plain link, no filled buttons. Confidence that the name and reputation carry the action. Avoids the “Book Now” pill button cliché.
4. **Deep specific historical narrative as content** — exact origin story (saloon 1900, French laundry 1920s, Schmitt 1978, Keller 1994), square footage, award years. Requires real institutional knowledge; not promptable generic “our story”.
5. **Rectangular, unshadowed, archival photography** — building exterior and awards rather than glossy plated dishes. Atmosphere from place and history, not appetite styling.
6. **Extreme minimalism in structure** — homepage is photo + logos + nav. Content lives on subpages. Feels like a permanent institution website, not a marketing landing page.

Honesto: sitio deliberadamente anticuado y seguro. El texto diminuto y las fuentes clásicas (Filosofia de Emigre, Ideal Sans de Hoefler) lo hacen sentir como un catálogo impreso de los 2000s más que una web moderna. No hay ni un solo botón filled o rounded. La copy es historia concreta del edificio y los premios, no promesas de “experiencia única”. Excelente referencia anti-IA para premium que quiere parecer permanente e institucional en lugar de “generado por template 2024”.

## Atelier Crenn (ateliercrenn.com) — premium independent, three-Michelin-star poetic fine dining, San Francisco (Dominique Crenn)

Investigated live site (desktop + mobile viewport) 5 November 2026. BentoBox platform. Dark slate, moody editorial. Currently featuring an ASKA collaboration banner.

### What was seen

- **Structure & CTAs**: Dark homepage with hero image carousel (6 slides). Top nav text-only (Information with sub-menu: Our Menu, FAQ, Continuum Series, Dietary Restrictions, Private Dining & Events, Crenn Collection, Gift Cards, Careers; Reservations). Prominent banner for ASKA collaboration (“Join us November 17 for an exclusive collaboration. RESERVE YOUR TABLE”). Primary reservation link goes to OpenTable. Footer has social, Bar Crenn, Bleu Belle Farm, email signup. No giant empty hero slogan; content is chef bio + gallery. Mobile uses toggle navigation.
- **Typography**: Headings: Julius Sans One (display geometric sans), 36–54px, weight 700, light gray rgb(190,197,200). Body and most UI: Halant (serif), ~18px weight 400, same light gray. Nav uppercase. Literary/poetic feel from the serif body against the display headings.
- **Buttons**: Mostly plain text links (Reservations, nav) with border-radius 0, no fill, no shadow. One filled “Reserve Your Table” button: bg rgb(110,117,120), white text, border-radius 4px, padding 6px 16px, Julius Sans One ~14.4px weight 700, uppercase. Social icons are circular. Very little button chrome.
- **Colors**: Dark slate background rgb(74,83,87). Text light gray/off-white. Mid-gray accents rgb(110,117,120) on buttons and icons. No warm cream, no bright brand colors, no gradients. Color lives in the photography.
- **Spacing / borders / shadows / photography**: Clean spacing. Images rectangular, border-radius 0, no box-shadow. Gallery of restaurant/food/atmosphere shots. Feels dark, moody, editorial rather than bright lifestyle.
- **Menu / mobile / copy**: Menu page (our-menu) has no itemized list or prices — instead a long narrative: “Dining at Atelier Crenn is a singular experience of creativity, discovery, and connection… Born and raised in France, Crenn spent many summers in Brittany… After more than thirty years in California… Bleu Belle Farm in Sonoma… farmers, fishermen, ranchers…” Specific, poetic, place- and memory-bound. Mobile has toggle nav; design is dark and readable but desktop-oriented. AudioEye accessibility overlay present.

### Patterns useful for anti-IA webs

1. **Dark slate canvas + mixed display sans (Julius Sans One) + literary serif body (Halant)** — moody, almost nocturnal editorial feel instead of white + Inter or cream + soft rounded type.
2. **Mostly text links + one modest filled button (radius 4px, mid-gray)** — restraint over big rounded filled CTAs. Confidence in the name and the OpenTable link.
3. **Poetic, highly specific chef narrative as menu and homepage content** — Brittany summers, 30+ years in California, Bleu Belle Farm, named collaborators, memory/place language. Not generic “culinary journey” copy; requires real biography.
4. **Rectangular unshadowed gallery photography on dark ground** — atmosphere from the real room and plates, not floating stock food on white.
5. **Minimal mid-gray accents only** — dark bg + light text + one gray button. Luxury from type contrast and restraint, not color pops or shadows.
6. **Honest institutional/poetic voice** — exact Michelin milestones, personal geography, farm name, no filler hospitality language.

Honesto: sitio oscuro, quieto y muy seguro de su voz poética. El combo Julius Sans One + Halant sobre slate lo aleja completamente de los templates AI claros con botones pill grandes. La copy del menú es biografía concreta, no marketing genérico. Buena referencia para premium independiente que quiera sentirse editorial y personal en lugar de “generado en 2024”.

## Septime (septime-charonne.fr) — premium-casual independent, modern bistro / neo-bistro, Paris 11 (Bertrand Grébaut)

Investigated live site (desktop + mobile viewport) 5 November 2026. Custom fullpage.js site. Extremely minimal, typewriter-driven, almost no photography. White background, black text, Prestige monospace font throughout. Homepage is logo + fullpage sections for menu, contacts, hours, vignerons.

### What was seen

- **Structure & CTAs**: Top news bar “SITE OFFICIEL - septime-charonne.fr”. Nav is plain text links (Menu d'automne, Contacts & réservation, Horaires d'ouverture, Les Vignerons, Boutique, Clamato, Septime La Cave, Tapisserie, press/jobs emails, Instagram). Primary CTA is “RÉSERVER” (text or black filled). Cover section is just the logo (SVG). Sections stack via fullpage scroll. Reservations note: open every day at 10h for the next 3 weeks (J+21). Mobile viewport (375px) keeps the same text structure and reserve buttons visible.
- **Typography**: Entire site uses Prestige (typewriter/monospace), body ~14px weight 300, color black. Headings same family, some 22px weight 300. Nav links ~17px. No serif/sans mix; pure monospaced typewriter feel. Letter-spacing normal. Extremely consistent and analog.
- **Buttons**: Mostly plain text links (radius 0, no bg, no border, no shadow). “RÉSERVER” appears as filled black bg rgb(0,0,0), white text, border-radius 0, padding ~8px 14px, no shadow. Sharp rectangular. High contrast, no rounded pills or outlines.
- **Colors**: Pure white background, pure black text. No accents, no gradients, no warm tones on UI. Color is absent except in the logo and any external links. Extremely high contrast, institutional/typewriter palette.
- **Spacing / borders / shadows / photography**: Tight, text-dense sections. Almost zero photography: only the logo image (radius 0, no shadow). No food, no interior, no people. Borders and shadows absent. Atmosphere comes from the type and the long list of producer names, not images.
- **Menu / mobile / copy**: Menu is only tasting menus: “Menu carte blanche en 5 étapes 85” (lunch), “7 étapes 135” (dinner) + wine pairings. No itemized dishes. Hours: lundi—vendredi 12h15-14h et 19h30-23h, closed weekends. Copy is purely operational: address 80 rue de Charonne Paris 11, reservation window, gift card note, long wall of vigneron names (Ganevat, Pfifferling, Houillon, Belluard, etc.). Language is factual, French, specific, zero marketing fluff. Mobile readable as stacked text; fullpage may feel linear on phone.

### Patterns useful for anti-IA webs

1. **Typewriter monospace (Prestige) as the sole typeface on white** — analog, analog-printer feel instead of Inter/system-ui or soft serif. Immediately non-template, non-AI-default.
2. **Sharp rectangular black filled buttons (radius 0) + mostly plain text links** — no pills, no shadows, no outlines. Confidence through restraint and monospaced type.
3. **Almost zero photography + operational density** — logo only, content is hours, exact reservation rule (every day 10h for J+21), tasting menu prices, and a wall of real producer names. Atmosphere from specificity, not food porn.
4. **Fullpage sections as structure** — logo cover, then menu/contacts/hours/vignerons. Feels like a printed sheet or old personal site, not a modern landing page with hero + cards.
5. **Honest, place-and-process voice** — exact address, hours, reservation mechanics, named vignerons. No “culinary journey” or “unique experience” language; pure operational truth.
6. **Pure black/white high-contrast monochrome** — no palette beyond type. Luxury/reputation signal from the typewriter font and the refusal to decorate.

Honesto: sitio casi arcaico y deliberadamente anti-visual. El monospace Prestige sobre blanco lo hace sentir como una máquina de escribir o un sitio personal de 2005 más que una web de restaurante 2020s. Casi no hay fotos (solo logo). El contenido es puramente operativo: precios de menú degustación, regla exacta de reservas, lista interminable de vignerons reales. Excelente referencia anti-IA para premium-casual que quiere parecer humano, específico y no generado por template con fotos de stock y botones redondeados.

## Asador Etxebarri (asadoretxebarri.com) — premium independent, wood-fire grill / asador, Axpe Bizkaia (Bittor Arginzoniz) — World's 50 Best #1 2026

Investigated live site (desktop + mobile viewport) 5 November 2026. Webflow. Extremely operational, quiet, almost no marketing. Warm cream canvas, thin display type, sharp black buttons. Homepage is pure booking mechanics and rules. No food photography, no chef story, no “experience” language.

### What was seen

- **Structure & CTAs**: Top nav text links (Reservations, Where to find us, Contact) + language switcher (Español / Euskera / English). Homepage opens with “How it works” (small) then large thin heading “Booking requests”. Content is sequential operational blocks: progressive calendar opening, request process, volume disclaimer, then icon+text cards for Price 300€ (tax incl., drinks extra, full prepayment), Meat and fish (no veg/vegan, tasting or à la carte full table), Guests (min 2 max 6, children from 12, no pets except guide dogs), Lunch service (12:30/13:00 only, closed Mon/Tue, closed 24 Dec–10 Jan and all August). Primary CTA “Submit Booking Request” (filled black). Then location with map image, FAQs accordion, contact form. Footer copyright 2021 + legal. Mobile (375px) keeps same structure, h1 scales to ~50px, CTAs remain.
- **Typography**: Body and most UI: Untitled Sans, 14–17px weight 400, color dark gray or muted. Small section labels (How it works, Where to find us): Untitled Sans 15px, color taupe/beige rgb(183,162,144). Large display headings (“Booking requests”, “Location”): Canela (display), 67px desktop / 50px mobile, weight 100 (extremely thin), color near-black rgb(21,21,19). Nav ~16–17px. High contrast but restrained; thin display creates elegance without weight.
- **Buttons**: Mostly plain text links (radius 0, no fill, no shadow). Main CTA “Submit Booking Request”: filled bg rgb(21,21,19), white text, border-radius 0, padding 8px 48px, border 1px solid same color, font 15px weight 400, no shadow. Sharp rectangular. Secondary text links. High contrast, zero pill or outline styles.
- **Colors**: Warm cream/off-white background rgb(250,248,243). Near-black text rgb(21,21,19). Taupe accent rgb(183,162,144) for small labels. White on black for the filled button. No bright colors, no gradients. Extremely quiet palette; color almost only from the map and simple line icons.
- **Spacing / borders / shadows / photography**: Clean operational spacing. Icons are simple line drawings (meat, people, clock). Map image is rectangular, radius 0, no shadow. Almost zero photography: logo, icons, one map. No food, no interior, no people, no fire/grill shots on the homepage. Borders and shadows absent. Atmosphere comes from the rules and the place name, not images.
- **Menu / mobile / copy**: No menu page or dishes listed (request-only, tasting or full-table à la carte). Copy is purely factual and restrictive: exact price 300€, “We do not have vegetarian and/or vegan menu”, min/max guests, lunch only, specific closed periods, request process (confirmation within 7 days or waitlist, prepayment, cancel ≥5 days or no refund, no-show no refund). Language is direct, Spanish/English operational, zero hospitality fluff. Mobile stacks cleanly and remains readable.

### Patterns useful for anti-IA webs

1. **Warm cream canvas + extremely thin display type (Canela weight 100 at 67px) + Untitled Sans body** — quiet elegance from thin letterforms on beige instead of bold geometric sans or rounded soft type. Anti-AI default of heavy weights and Inter.
2. **Sharp rectangular black filled button (radius 0, generous horizontal padding) + mostly text links** — no pills, no shadows, no outlines. Confidence through restraint and the operational CTA.
3. **Pure operational density as homepage content** — exact price, dietary restrictions, guest limits, service hours, closed dates, request/prepayment/cancellation rules. Requires real policy knowledge; not generatable as generic “book your table” marketing.
4. **Almost zero photography + simple line icons + map** — no food porn, no lifestyle shots. Atmosphere and desire come from the reputation and the specific rules, not images.
5. **Taupe accent only on small labels + cream/black core** — minimal palette. Luxury signal from type thinness, restraint, and specificity rather than color or decoration.
6. **Honest, restrictive, place-bound voice** — “We do not have vegetarian…”, exact closed periods, 7-day response window, prepayment, 5-day cancel rule. Transparency about limitations and process instead of aspirational copy.

Honesto: sitio deliberadamente quieto y operativo. El tipo Canela ultra-fino (weight 100) sobre crema rgb(250,248,243) lo hace sentir como un documento institucional o un formulario de reserva más que una web de restaurante. Casi no hay fotos (logo, iconos, mapa). El contenido es puro reglamento: 300€, sin vegano, máximo 6, solo comida, cerrado agosto, prepayment, etc. Excelente referencia anti-IA para premium que quiere parecer seguro de su posición (#1 World's 50 Best) y no necesita venderse con fotos de platos ni copy de “experiencia única a la brasa”.
