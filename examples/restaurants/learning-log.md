## Noma (noma.dk) — premium independent, New Nordic fine dining / food lab, Copenhagen (René Redzepi)

Investigated live site (desktop) 5 November 2026. WordPress, quiet editorial with warm cream canvas and serif type. Currently focused on Copenhagen return and Noma LA residency.

### What was seen

- **Structure & CTAs**: Home opens with large heading “Copenhagen 2026-2027” and “Book a table”. Long narrative letter about coming home after LA residency, new leadership (Mette Brink Søberg, Pablo Soto, Annika de Las Heras), twelve seasons rhythm. Sections for Noma Projects shop, Noma Kaffe, book. Nav is minimal text (Reservations, Menu). Footer has contact (Refshalevej 96, phone, hours). No giant empty hero slogan; the letter is the content. Mobile would stack cleanly; “Book a table” remains visible.
- **Typography**: Body and headings: Reckless / Reckless-Neue (serif), body ~18px weight 400, headings 25–56px weight 400–500, color dark green-brown rgb(41,56,43). Some nav/UI labels use Roobert (sans). Strong literary serif feel; not geometric sans.
- **Buttons**: Mostly text links or sharp-corner (border-radius 0). Cookie “Accept all” is filled dark green-brown on cream. “Book a table” / “Reservations” are plain text or light. No heavy rounded filled CTAs or shadows. Contrast high via type.
- **Colors**: Warm cream/off-white background rgb(249,249,243). Dark green-brown text. No bright accents or gradients. Color lives in photography (ingredients, spaces, products).
- **Spacing / borders / shadows / photography**: Generous spacing around narrative blocks. Photos rectangular, border-radius 0, no box-shadow. Documentary of real places, ingredients, books, coffee. Feels like a journal or research lab announcement rather than restaurant marketing.
- **Menu / mobile / copy**: No static menu (seasonal, operational). Copy is highly specific and institutional: exact dates of LA residency (March 11–June 26), named new leaders and roles, “twelve seasons”, gratitude for foragers/ceramicists/fishermen, “It feels good to be coming home.” Language is precise, reflective, not generic hospitality filler. Contact and booking paths clear.

### Patterns useful for anti-IA webs

1. **Warm cream canvas + consistent literary serif (Reckless)** — book/journal feel on beige instead of white + Inter. Avoids AI template geometric sans + rounded everything.
2. **Typography and plain text as primary CTA** — “Book a table” as text or light link; sharp radius 0 on any filled elements. Quiet confidence over button chrome.
3. **Long specific narrative letter as homepage content** — detailed operational and leadership update with real names, dates, and reflections. Requires deep knowledge; not generatable from a generic prompt.
4. **Rectangular unshadowed documentary photography** — ingredients, spaces, products. Atmosphere from the real institution and research, not stock food close-ups.
5. **Minimal palette, color from photos** — cream + dark green-brown. Luxury/research signal from type, restraint, and specificity rather than decorative UI.
6. **Honest institutional voice** — naming new leaders, seasonal structure, past challenges, specific collaborators. Transparency and place/time-bound language.

Honesto: sitio muy quieto y seguro de su reputación. El tipo serif Reckless sobre crema lo hace sentir como un anuncio o carta institucional más que una web de restaurante template. La fotografía es de productos, ingredientes y espacios reales. Ideal referencia para premium independiente con voz fuerte que no caiga en el cliché AI de botones redondeados filled grandes, white space vacío y copy genérico de “experiencia culinaria única”.
